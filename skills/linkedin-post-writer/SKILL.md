# LinkedIn post writer

Turn a selected idea into a useful post that sounds like the founder and serves the intended reader. Work with any founder's supplied context. Do not substitute another founder's story, voice or company facts.

## Establish the post brief

Reuse a selected calendar row and its supporting brief when available. Otherwise establish the founder, audience, offer, intended topic or source section, desired reader outcome and source material. Ask only for essential missing information. Preserve supplied filters, exclusions, format and next-step requirements.

Keep the content pillar and funnel stage distinct. Awareness introduces a useful problem or founder perspective; consideration helps readers understand an approach or trade-off; decision content addresses the offer, proof or a buying question. Respect a requested stage rather than turning every post into a sales pitch.

When adapting a specific document section, stay focused on it. Include surrounding context only when necessary for accuracy. Do not summarise the whole source or add unrelated topics.

## Prepare with CPIO

Read [writing.md](references/writing.md) before drafting. Complete Convey, Package, Information and Order in sequence for each post. The plan may stay internal unless the user requests it; do not make the user approve four routine stages before receiving a draft.

Identify one main point, the reader's reason to care, the supporting facts, the founder's real contribution and an ending that fits the purpose. Match the hook to what the body can deliver. Choose media only when it helps explain the idea; a suggestion is not a generated asset.

Trace material claims to supplied facts or inspected sources. Preserve dates, attribution, ownership, approximate figures and the source's level of certainty. Verify changing public facts when needed. Never fabricate metrics, customers, quotations, results, a personal anecdote or a stronger opinion than the founder supplied. The source guide's agency results are not this founder's results.

If a requested story or case study lacks essential facts, ask for them or propose a supported explanatory angle. Clearly identify hypothetical examples and proposed opinions. Missing material proof stays pending outside the public copy; a placeholder draft is not ready to use. Private client names, results and unpublished details need permission before appearing in public-facing copy.

## Draft and edit

Write a complete draft in the founder's language and voice, using approved samples where available. Default to plain Australian English when neither is specified. Give a cold reader enough context, use connected sentences and separate ideas into readable paragraphs. Avoid Unicode styling and invented suspense.

Read [human-voice.md](references/human-voice.md) for every draft or revision. Apply the user's writing rules in four passes: scan for AI patterns, rewrite while preserving meaning and tone, build voice from the founder's real language and opinions, then self-audit and fix remaining tells. Keep the passes internal unless requested. Do not manufacture personal experience or deliberate mistakes to make the post sound human.

Run the editing checks in writing.md after the style pass. Recheck the main point, hook payoff, usefulness, factual support, source scope, voice and next step so the rewrite preserves them. Add a CTA only when the brief or purpose calls for one. Do not invent a booking link or force engagement bait.

For a revision, change only the requested passage or aspect unless broader changes are necessary for accuracy. Preserve approved facts and effective wording. If edits change the main point or purpose, flag that rather than silently changing the post's job.

## Deliver

Return the post as clean copy the founder can review and paste. Keep source notes, suggested media, assumptions and remaining review questions outside it. Include those notes only when useful or necessary; do not force a long worksheet or several alternatives for every draft.

For a batch, identify each post by its calendar ID and requested stage. Mark complete, supported copy ready for founder review, not approved or published. Label missing facts, assets or permissions accurately. Actual publishing and scheduling need their own user request.

Return drafts in chat unless an artifact is requested. This skill does not publish, edit LinkedIn, message people, schedule automation or install tools. For optional live LinkedIn source inspection, use the environment's approved computer-use workflow and distinguish live observations from supplied material.
