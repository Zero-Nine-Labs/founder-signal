# CPIO and editing

## C: Convey

State the one thing the reader should understand after the post. Tie it to the buyer question, selected topic and requested funnel stage. Name the useful job of the post, such as explaining a decision, teaching a process, sharing a supported lesson or answering an offer question.

Find the source material that supports that point. If it cannot support the requested conclusion, narrow the conclusion or ask for the missing input. A stronger hook does not justify a stronger claim.

## P: Package

Choose the angle, format and opening to suit the material:

| Material | Possible package |
|---|---|
| Actual founder experience | Story with enough context to understand its lesson |
| Process or framework | Explanation or steps with a practical takeaway |
| Trade-off or supported opinion | Clear position with reasons and an example |
| Verified case study | Starting situation, work done, result, limitations and evidence |
| Accurate buying FAQ | Direct answer, relevant detail and a feasible next step |
| Timely industry source | Source-grounded commentary explaining the effect for the audience |

A carousel, screenshot, founder photo or video can help when it clarifies the idea. Text alone is valid. Never describe suggested media as an existing asset, and do not generate it unless requested.

Make the opening's value clear to the right reader and give them a reason to continue. The body must deliver the promised answer. Do not manufacture numbers, authority, suspense or a personal confession to improve attention.

## I: Information

Identify the facts, examples and explanations necessary to complete the point. Exclude related material that weakens its focus. Record essential missing facts before drafting.

Keep a distinction between documented outcomes, the founder's own observations or opinions, general explanations and hypothetical examples. A demonstration is not a production customer result. An account metric is not proof that a post created a sale. Keep approximate numbers approximate and do not invent causal links between separate facts.

Source material can be private without being approved for publication. Use only permitted public details in the post. Do not turn confidential notes into testimonials or imply permission from the mere availability of a transcript.

## O: Order

Arrange the information for a reader who does not already know the founder or company. Establish the idea's value, add necessary context, develop the answer and put evidence near the claim it supports. Finish when the point is complete.

The order depends on the package, not a compulsory story template. A process needs its steps in a useful sequence. A case study needs its starting conditions before a result can be understood. Avoid a repeated conclusion or an unrelated CTA.

## Editing checks

Before delivery, confirm:

- One clear point, within the selected topic or source section and at the intended funnel stage.
- The body delivers the hook's promise and gives the reader something useful.
- A cold reader has enough context; unnecessary material is removed.
- Claims, figures, roles and quotations have support and correct attribution.
- Examples are specific and accurately labelled, including hypothetical ones.
- The wording preserves the founder's actual opinion and level of certainty.
- Each paragraph adds useful information. Remove restatements, including voice-sample lines copied into the post without adding to its point.
- Connected sentences and natural paragraphs are readable on mobile without artificial line breaks after every sentence.
- Wording and formatting pass the user-supplied rules in [human-voice.md](human-voice.md), including the final self-audit for remaining AI patterns.
- Any CTA follows the brief and uses a real, supported route.
- Remaining factual gaps or permissions are explicit outside the draft; unresolved placeholders are not labelled ready to use.

If no voice samples exist, use plain language and flag voice calibration only when it matters. Do not pretend to have reproduced a demonstrated personal style. A useful post does not need a fixed length or fabricated personal experience.
